Rules
  • Arms, given center C: Half 0.5C, Near-low C/1.1, Near-high 1.1C, Double 2C.
  • Floor: for k > 0, elasticity e = −ln(k) / ln(2). A report must satisfy q(p′) ≥ q(p) · (p′/p)−e whenever p′ > p. k = 0 is no floor. Upward-sloping reports are allowed.
  • Honest type: fraction h of true demand at the highest price. Each lower price bids as much as the floor allows, and no more than h·D(p).
  • Shade type: fraction 1−h sits entirely on the cheapest arm. Higher arms sit on the floor from that quantity, or at 0 when k = 0.
  • The winner is the arm with the greatest revenue p·Q. Revenues within $0.0001 count as a tie. Ties go to the arm closer to C, then to the lower price.
  • The optimal fee maximizes p·D(p) on a 200-price log grid from one sixteenth of the lowest listed price to sixteen times the highest, with every listed price included so a kink is not missed. A constant reporter cost would not move that price, so it is omitted.
  • Market creator fee is a percent of open interest per month. The slider is logarithmic. It rescales the prices in the table, and the auction uses those prices.
  • Percent of Polymarket demand scales every quantity. 100% is the full curve. That scale does not move the optimal fee.
  • The controller must move to one of the four prices. A month works when it selects the price that maximizes revenue when every bidder is honest.
  • The honesty threshold is the smallest honest share, on a 0.1% grid and with no bluff-caller, at which that price wins. With a bluff-caller helping, the auction fails to select it only when the honest share is below a second, lower cutoff.
  • Controller efficiency is this month’s burn at the winning price, p·D(p), divided by the maximum REP burn.
  • A bluff-caller bids nothing on the cheaper prices and bids on one higher price. They buy at least enough to lead the book by $0.01 of revenue, and more when the extra tokens still resell above their cost. Other bidders create the highest-value markets with the tokens they get at that price. The caller resells to the less valuable markets left over, at no more than the revenue-maximizing fee. They choose the purchase price, the quantity, and the resale price with the most profit. If that profit is positive, their bid is in the book and that price wins.
  • Next Month copies this month’s winning price into the current query fee.
